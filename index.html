Good morning, my love.

If you are reading this, you’ve probably just woken up—rubbing your eyes, maybe wrapping yourself in your blanket for five extra minutes before the world asks for your energy. Before your phone fills up with notifications or your mind races to today’s to-do list, I wanted this little space to be waiting for you.

I want to start with something honest and important: I am really sorry. Lately, I have not been giving you enough time. Between work, obligations, and letting myself get pulled in too many directions, I haven't been as present as you deserve. And I know that when I become distant or distracted, it doesn’t just mean fewer phone calls—it can make you feel unseen, unvalued, or like you’re carrying the emotional weight of us on your own.

I hate that I ever made you feel that way. You never deserve the leftover scraps of my energy at the end of an exhausting day. You deserve a boyfriend who looks up from his screen, who listens to what you’re feeling without being in a rush, who remembers the little things you mention in passing, and who actively carves out sacred, uninterrupted time for the two of us.

This website isn’t an excuse, and I don’t expect a single gesture or pretty sentences to instantly fix the days I let slip by. Real care isn't proven by a big gesture when you're hurt—it’s proven by consistency, by checking in on your heart, by keeping promises, and by showing up every ordinary Tuesday just as much as on special days.

I made this little universe for you so that whenever you look at it, you’re reminded of how deeply noticed, cherished, and important you are to me. Take your time wandering through it. Every word here was written thinking of you.

with all my love,
always yours, Sahil ♡

the little things I love about you

It’s never just the big things. It’s the sweet, quiet habits you probably don’t even realize you have. The way your voice softens when you’re sleepy. The cute face you make when you're trying to hide that you found my dumb joke funny.

It’s how your hand instinctively reaches for mine when we walk beside each other, and how you tuck yourself under my arm like you were designed to fit there. Those tiny, unscripted moments are my favorite parts of living.

✨

things I hope you never stop seeing in yourself

On days when you feel exhausted or critical of yourself, I wish I could give you my perspective for just a few minutes. I see a woman who carries incredible kindness, who gives so much of her warmth to everyone around her.

I see someone with rare resilience—capable, intelligent, and deeply genuine. Never let a tough week or self-doubt trick you into forgetting how extraordinary you are.

☕

the comfort you bring me

The world can be loud, chaotic, and demanding, but being near you quiets everything down. When I’ve had the longest day and my thoughts are running in circles, just hearing your voice or having your head on my chest brings a stillness I can’t find anywhere else.

You are my calm harbor. Home isn't an address or a set of walls for me—it’s wherever you are.

💌

things I might not say often enough

Thank you for being in my corner. Thank you for your patience with me, even when I have been foolish, quiet, or preoccupied. Thank you for making ordinary days feel memorable simply by sharing them with me.

I don’t ever want you to wonder whether I see how much you bring into my life. I notice it every day, and I am grateful for you more than words can express.

☀️

little everyday moments I want more of

I don’t need extravagant holidays to be happy with you. What I want is more slow Sunday mornings where neither of us has to set an alarm. I want to make you breakfast, bring you warm tea just the way you like it, and sit together talking about everything and nothing.

I want comfortable silences where we just read or work side by side, knowing that the best person in the world is sitting right next to me.

🌱

why I choose to make room for us

You are not an obligation to be squeezed into the gaps of a busy schedule. You are the reason the work is worth doing in the first place. Making deliberate time for us isn’t a chore or a compromise—it is the best part of my week.

I choose you when life is easy, and I choose you when life is overwhelming. You are my priority, and I am putting my actions behind that truth.

🌌

things I want us to experience together

There are so many places I want to discover with you. Stargazing far away from city lights, wrapped up in a thick blanket. Spontaneous road trips where we stop at weird roadside diners and laugh until our ribs ache.

Cooking new recipes together that might turn out terrible, but won’t matter because we’ll just end up ordering pizza and eating it on the floor. I want a lifetime of memories with you.

🤍

what to remember on a difficult day

When everything feels heavy and you feel like you aren't doing enough, please remember: your worth is not measured by productivity or perfection. You are allowed to take a break. You are allowed to be vulnerable.

You never have to pretend to be cheerful around me. My arms will always be a safe shelter where you can take off your armor and just be.

🕊️

a letter for when you miss me

Whenever miles or busy hours stand between us, remember that distance is only physical. In every quiet thought, in every playlist I listen to, in every little thing that makes me smile throughout my day, you are right there with me.

Whenever you feel lonely, look at this screen, read these words, and know that you are held tightly in my heart every single second.

🛡️

a letter for when you need reassurance

If your mind ever whispers that you are asking for too much or that I might slip away: let me quiet that fear immediately. You are not a burden. Asking for love, time, and attention is the bare minimum you deserve.

My affection for you isn't temporary or fickle. I am here, I am listening, and I am in this with you with my whole heart.

👑

a final note just for you

Out of eight billion people wandering across this planet, with all their different paths and stories, my favorite thing that ever happened was meeting you.

You have brought so much color, warmth, and gentleness into my world. Thank you for being my girlfriend, my best friend, and my favorite part of every day. I love you, completely and without hesitation.
